# Buttered Noodles

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 12 min
**Tags:** dinner, pasta, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450 g) egg noodles
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- Salt and pepper
- Chopped parsley (optional)

## Steps

1. Cook the noodles in salted boiling water until tender. Drain, saving a splash of pasta water.
2. Toss the hot noodles with the butter and Parmesan, adding pasta water if they look dry.
3. Season with salt and pepper and top with parsley.
