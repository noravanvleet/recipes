# Tacos

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 15 min
**Tags:** dinner, mexican, beef, weeknight

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450 g) ground beef
- 1 packet (1 oz) taco seasoning
- 2/3 cup water
- 8 taco shells or small tortillas
- 1 cup shredded cheddar
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 tomato, diced
- Sour cream and salsa, for serving

## Steps

1. Brown the beef in a skillet over medium-high heat and drain the fat.
2. Stir in the taco seasoning and water and simmer until thickened, about 5 minutes.
3. Warm the shells or tortillas.
4. Fill with beef and top with cheese, lettuce, tomato, sour cream, and salsa.
