# Pierogies

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 20 min
**Tags:** dinner, potato, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450 g) frozen potato and cheese pierogies
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
- Salt and pepper
- Sour cream, for serving

## Steps

1. Boil the pierogies in salted water until they float, about 5 minutes. Drain.
2. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat and cook the onion until soft and golden, about 10 minutes.
3. Add the pierogies and fry until browned on both sides.
4. Season with salt and pepper and serve with sour cream.
