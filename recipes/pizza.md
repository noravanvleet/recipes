# Pizza

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 15 min
**Tags:** dinner, pizza

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450 g) pizza dough
- 1/2 cup pizza sauce
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella
- Toppings of choice (e.g. pepperoni)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Flour or cornmeal, for dusting

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
2. Stretch the dough on a floured or cornmeal-dusted pan and brush with olive oil.
3. Spread the sauce, then add the cheese and toppings.
4. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese bubbles, 12 to 15 minutes.
