# Breakfast

**Serves:** 2 · **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 15 min
**Tags:** breakfast, eggs

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 4 slices bacon
- 2 slices bread, for toast
- 1 tbsp butter
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Cook the bacon in a skillet until crisp, then drain on paper towels.
2. Toast the bread and butter it.
3. Cook the eggs in the skillet the way you like them and season with salt and pepper.
4. Serve together.
