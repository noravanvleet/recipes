# Pasta Salad

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 10 min
**Tags:** side, pasta, cold

## Ingredients

- 12 oz (340 g) rotini
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1/2 cup sliced black olives
- 4 oz (115 g) salami, diced
- 4 oz (115 g) mozzarella pearls
- 3/4 cup Italian dressing

## Steps

1. Cook the pasta, rinse under cold water, and drain well.
2. Toss with the vegetables, olives, salami, and mozzarella.
3. Stir in the dressing and chill for at least 30 minutes before serving.
