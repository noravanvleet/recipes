# Mac and Cheese

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 20 min
**Tags:** dinner, pasta, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 8 oz (225 g) elbow macaroni
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp flour
- 2 cups milk
- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Cook the macaroni in salted boiling water and drain.
2. Melt the butter in a saucepan, whisk in the flour, and cook for 1 minute.
3. Slowly whisk in the milk and simmer until thickened.
4. Take off the heat, stir in the cheese until melted, season, and stir in the macaroni.
