# Hot Dog

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 2 min · **Cook:** 8 min
**Tags:** lunch, quick, grill

## Ingredients

- 4 hot dogs
- 4 hot dog buns
- Ketchup, mustard, and relish, for serving

## Steps

1. Grill, pan-fry, or boil the hot dogs until heated through.
2. Warm the buns.
3. Serve in the buns with your toppings.
