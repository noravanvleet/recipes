# Grilled Cheese

**Serves:** 1 · **Prep:** 2 min · **Cook:** 8 min
**Tags:** lunch, sandwich, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 2 slices bread
- 2 slices cheddar or American cheese
- 1 tbsp butter, softened

## Steps

1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
2. Put one slice butter-side down in a skillet over medium-low heat, add the cheese, and top with the other slice butter-side up.
3. Cook until golden and the cheese melts, 3 to 4 minutes per side.
