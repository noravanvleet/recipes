# Tomato Cream Pasta

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 20 min
**Tags:** dinner, pasta, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 12 oz (340 g) penne
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- Salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes
- Fresh basil, for serving

## Steps

1. Cook the pasta, saving 1/2 cup of pasta water.
2. Warm the oil in a large pan and cook the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes, then stir in the cream and Parmesan.
4. Season, toss with the pasta, and loosen with pasta water as needed. Top with basil.
