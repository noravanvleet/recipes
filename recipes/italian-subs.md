# Italian Subs

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 0 min
**Tags:** lunch, sandwich, no-cook

## Ingredients

- 4 sub rolls
- 1/4 lb (115 g) salami
- 1/4 lb (115 g) ham
- 1/4 lb (115 g) capicola
- 1/4 lb (115 g) provolone
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp dried oregano

## Steps

1. Split the rolls.
2. Layer the meats and provolone, then the lettuce, tomato, and onion.
3. Whisk the oil, vinegar, and oregano and drizzle over the filling.
