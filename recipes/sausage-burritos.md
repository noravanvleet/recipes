# Sausage Burritos

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 15 min
**Tags:** breakfast, sausage, eggs, make-ahead

## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb (225 g) breakfast sausage
- 6 eggs
- 1 cup shredded cheddar
- 4 large flour tortillas
- Salsa, for serving

## Steps

1. Brown the sausage in a skillet, breaking it up, and drain.
2. Add the beaten eggs and scramble until just set.
3. Divide the filling and cheese among the tortillas and roll them up.
4. Serve with salsa, or wrap and freeze for later.
