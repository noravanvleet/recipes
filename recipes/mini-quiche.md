# Mini Quiche

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 20 min
**Tags:** breakfast, eggs, make-ahead

## Ingredients

- 1 refrigerated pie crust (or 12 mini tart shells)
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup cooked diced ham or bacon
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
2. Cut the crust into rounds and press them into the cups.
3. Whisk the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper. Divide the cheese and ham among the cups and pour in the egg mixture.
4. Bake until set, 18 to 20 minutes.

## Notes

Makes 12 mini quiches.
