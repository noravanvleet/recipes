# Quesadilla

**Serves:** 2 · **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 10 min
**Tags:** lunch, mexican, quick, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 4 flour tortillas (8 inch)
- 2 cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese
- 1 tbsp butter or oil
- Salsa and sour cream, for serving

## Steps

1. Heat a skillet over medium heat and add a little butter.
2. Lay a tortilla in the pan, cover half with cheese, and fold it over.
3. Cook until golden and the cheese melts, 2 to 3 minutes per side.
4. Cut into wedges and serve with salsa and sour cream.
