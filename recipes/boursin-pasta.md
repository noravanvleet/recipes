# Boursin Pasta

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** 30 min
**Tags:** dinner, pasta, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 2 pints (about 600 g) cherry tomatoes
- 1 package (5.2 oz) Boursin garlic and herb cheese
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 12 oz (340 g) pasta
- Salt and pepper
- Fresh basil, for serving

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Put the Boursin in the middle of a baking dish, surround it with the tomatoes and garlic, drizzle with olive oil, and season.
3. Bake until the tomatoes burst, about 25 minutes. Meanwhile, cook the pasta.
4. Stir the cheese and tomatoes into a sauce, toss with the pasta, and top with basil.
