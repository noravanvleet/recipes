# Green Bean Italian Sausage Red Potato Dish

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 35 min
**Tags:** dinner, sausage, sheet-pan

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450 g) Italian sausage links, cut into chunks
- 1 1/2 lb (680 g) red potatoes, quartered
- 1 lb (450 g) green beans, trimmed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and seasonings on a sheet pan and roast for 15 minutes.
3. Add the sausage and green beans with the rest of the oil and seasonings.
4. Roast until the potatoes are tender and the sausage is cooked through, about 20 minutes more.
