# Squash Pasta

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 40 min
**Tags:** dinner, pasta, vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 small butternut squash (about 2 lb / 900 g), peeled and cubed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 12 oz (340 g) pasta
- Salt and pepper
- A few fresh sage leaves (optional)

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the squash with oil, salt, and pepper and roast until tender, about 30 minutes.
2. Cook the pasta, saving 1 cup of pasta water.
3. Blend the squash with the garlic, cream, Parmesan, and a splash of pasta water until smooth.
4. Toss the sauce with the pasta, thinning with more pasta water as needed. Top with sage.
