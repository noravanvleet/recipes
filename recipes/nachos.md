# Nachos

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 10 min
**Tags:** snack, mexican, quick

## Ingredients

- 1 bag (10 oz) tortilla chips
- 1/2 lb (225 g) ground beef, cooked with taco seasoning
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
- 2 cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese
- 1 jalapeño, sliced
- Salsa, sour cream, and guacamole, for serving

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Spread the chips on a sheet pan and top with beef, beans, cheese, and jalapeño.
3. Bake until the cheese melts, 8 to 10 minutes.
4. Serve with salsa, sour cream, and guacamole.
